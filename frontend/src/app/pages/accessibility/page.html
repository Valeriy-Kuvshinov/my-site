Accessibility Statement

1. General Principles and Commitment

Valeriy Kuvshinov, doing business as Kuvshinov Software ("Company", "We", or "Us"), operates as an independent digital consultancy and development agency. Because we provide professional accessibility remediation services, we hold our own digital infrastructure to the highest standards of digital inclusivity. We act to the full extent of our technical capabilities to ensure our platform provides an equal, seamless experience for all web users, including individuals with visual, motor, or cognitive disabilities.

This statement applies strictly to the digital properties operating under the domain root at kuvware.com. As a fully distributed digital entity, the Company operates no physical commercial offices open to the public; consequently, physical access metrics are non-applicable to our operational scope.

2. Digital Accessibility Standards & "No Overlay" Policy

The digital structure of this website has been engineered from the ground up—using native, semantic code—to achieve functional alignment with the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA and AAA conformance.

We strictly oppose the use of automated "accessibility overlays" or temporary widget patches. Instead, inclusivity is built directly into the DOM, ensuring structural interoperability with native assistive technologies, including screen readers, refreshable braille displays, and alternative input hardware.

3. Implemented Accessibility Controls

The core website architecture utilizes standard programmatic modifications alongside an integrated native accessibility menu, allowing users to execute real-time structural adaptations:

  • Semantic Document Geometry: Strict hierarchical heading order (H1 — H6), correct ARIA 1.2 labeling, and semantic landmark segmentation (<main>, <nav>, <aside>) to facilitate fast screen reader index navigation.
  • Native Keyboard Navigation Protocol: Complete operational execution via standard keyboard loops, including custom focus traps on modals and highly visible focus indicators.
  • Skip Link Overrides: Accessible skip links placed at the absolute programmatic root level, allowing keyboard users to bypass layout directly to the main content or footer.
  • Color Contrast Optimization: Visual text layers and active interactive components maintain an enhanced luminance contrast ratio matching or exceeding strict AAA (7:1) thresholds.
  • Advanced Typography Toggles: An isolated native accessibility interface allowing user-driven configuration changes, including system sans-serif overrides for dyslexia assistance, progressive scaling for text size, line-height modifications, and letter-spacing vectors.
  • Motion Mutation Flags: Comprehensive system hooks to pause looping animations and CSS transition matrices, synchronized automatically with system-level hardware preferences (prefers-reduced-motion).

4. Programmatic Boundaries & Third-Party Platforms

Users navigating our digital infrastructure may encounter specific structural boundaries dictated by external runtime layers and third-party ecosystems:

  • Security Challenges (CAPTCHA): To protect against automated abuse, we utilize Cloudflare Turnstile. While Turnstile is designed to run invisibly and provide accessible fallbacks, strict browser privacy settings may occasionally force a visual challenge.
  • External Platform Redirection: This website contains active links routing out to external ecosystems (including LinkedIn, GitHub, and client portfolio sites). These properties are maintained exclusively by their respective corporations or owners; consequently, their accessibility conformance levels remain entirely beyond our control.

5. Remediation, Feedback, and Contact Details

We maintain an ongoing codebase remediation protocol to continually identify and update accessibility anomalies. If you experience digital barriers, layout errors, or require alternative data delivery formats while browsing this site, please file an inquiry via our official communication channel here.

As part of our commitment to accessible engineering, we evaluate all accessibility inquiries rapidly and process structural modifications with the highest technical priority.
