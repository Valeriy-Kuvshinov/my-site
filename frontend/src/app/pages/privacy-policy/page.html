Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how "Did Not Get It" (the "Extension"), developed by vkfreelancesoftware, handles your personal information and data. Our core principle is absolute data minimization: your data belongs to you, stays on your device, and is never transmitted to our server.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

"Did Not Get It" operates entirely locally within your browser.

  • No Telemetry or Tracking: We do not track your browsing behavior, clicks, or extension usage.
  • No Server Transmissions: We do not own, operate, or route your data through any external backend servers.
  • No Email Harvesting: Your email content, sender addresses, and metadata are never copied, logged, or sent to the developer or any third party.

2. Google API Data Access and Usage

In order to function, the Extension requests access to your Gmail account using the Google OAuth 2.0 protocol, specifically to perform modifications in Gmail.

We access and use this data in the following strictly limited ways:

  • Reading Data: The Extension reads the HTML text of incoming unread emails locally in your browser to check for standard job rejection phrasing using regular expressions.
  • Modifying Data: If a rejection is detected, the Extension communicates directly with the Google Gmail API to apply your custom label and remove the "UNREAD" status from that specific email thread.
  • Authentication: Your Google OAuth access token is used exclusively to authorize these specific actions directly between your browser and Google's servers.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

"Did Not Get It" complies entirely with Google's strict requirements for accessing user data. "Did Not Get It"'s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We do not use your data to serve advertisements.
  • We do not allow humans to read your data.
  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data required for the Extension to function is stored securely and locally on your device using the browser's native browser.storage.local API. This includes:

  • Your custom rejection label text.
  • Your "rejections skipped" numerical counter.
  • Your current authentication state and extension settings (e.g., paused/active status).
  • The temporary OAuth access token provided by Google.

Uninstalling the Extension or clicking "Disconnect Gmail" inside the Extension will immediately clear this local data.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information, email data, or usage metrics with any third parties. The only data transmission that occurs is the direct communication between your browser and Google's official API endpoints.

6. User Control and Revocation

You have complete control over your data and the Extension's access to your account:

  • Pause Scanning: You can temporarily halt the Extension's access to your emails at any time using the "Pause Scanning" button in the Extension popup.
  • Disconnect: You can permanently sever the Extension's access by clicking "Disconnect Gmail" in the popup. This calls Google's official revocation API to invalidate your token and wipes all local authentication data.
  • Google Account Settings: You can review and revoke access to "Did Not Get It" at any time by visiting your Google account third-party apps.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to how we handle your data, we will update this Privacy Policy and update the "Effective Date" at the top of this page. Because the Extension does not collect your contact information, we recommend reviewing this policy periodically on the vkfreelancesoftware website.
