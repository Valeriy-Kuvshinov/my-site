Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected and how it is handled by Valeriy Kuvshinov, operating as Kuvshinov Software ("I" or "the Company"), on this website (kuvware.com) and in related software, including the "Did Not Get It" browser extension (together, "the Services").

1. Contact Form and Direct Messages

When you send a message through the contact form, I collect the name, email address, and message you provide. This is personal information. Please do not include passwords or sensitive details in your message.

I use this information only to respond to your inquiry and to provide support. Messages arrive in my business inbox. I never sell, rent, or share them with marketing companies. I keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your inquiry and for ordinary business records, and I delete it when you ask me to, unless the law requires me to keep it.

2. Analytics and Server Logs

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how it is used and to improve it. Analytics cookies are used only if you accept them in the cookie banner. Google may process this data on servers outside Israel.

The data is statistical and typically includes approximate location, browser and device details, and the pages you visit. It does not directly identify you, and I do not try to identify you with it. You can withdraw your consent at any time through the cookie settings on this site or by clearing this site's cookies and storage in your browser.

For security and reliability, this site is delivered through Cloudflare, which acts as a proxy and processes visitors' technical data such as IP addresses, browser details, and request times. My web server and hosting provider also keep standard technical logs. These logs are kept for a limited time, used only for security, abuse prevention, and fixing technical problems, and never for marketing or profiling.

3. Security and Bot Protection

To protect the site and its forms from spam and automated abuse, I use Cloudflare Turnstile, which works in the background to tell real visitors from bots. To do this, Cloudflare processes some technical information about your browser and connection. Your use of the forms is also subject to the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

4. Screen Reader Tutoring Sessions

Tutoring sessions run over video call (for example, Zoom through Fiverr, or directly). You share your screen, and I guide you verbally. I never ask for passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information, and you enter those yourself. Even so, sensitive information may sometimes appear on the screen you share.

I follow a no-recording, no-storage rule for session content: I do not record sessions, take screenshots, or save or copy anything I see on your screen. The written recaps I send after a session contain only navigation steps and a map of the site, never personal data. The video call platform (such as Zoom or Fiverr) handles data under its own privacy policy.

5. "Did Not Get It" Extension: Data Minimization

The "Did Not Get It" browser extension runs entirely inside your browser. Your data stays on your device and is never sent to me or to any server I operate.

  • No tracking: I do not track your browsing, clicks, or how you use the extension.
  • No servers: I do not run any backend servers that receive your data.
  • No Email collection: Your email content, sender addresses, and metadata are never copied, logged, or sent to me or to any third party.
  • Local storage: The extension stores only its own settings (such as your chosen label) and a counter of skipped rejections in your browser's storage. Removing the extension deletes this data.

6. Extension: Google API Data Access (Limited Use)

To work, the extension asks for access to your Gmail account through Google OAuth 2.0. It uses that access only in these limited ways:

  • Reading: The extension reads the text of incoming unread emails locally in your browser, to check for standard job-rejection phrasing.
  • Modifying: If a rejection is detected, the extension asks the Gmail API to apply your label and remove the "UNREAD" status from that email thread.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs by "Did Not Get It" adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The extension does not use your data for advertising, does not allow humans to read it, and does not sell or transfer it.

You can revoke the extension's access at any time in the third-party access settings of your Google Account, or by removing the extension.

7. External Links and Embedded Content

This site links to other websites and embeds a YouTube video. When you follow a link or play embedded content, that third party's privacy practices apply, and I have no control over them or responsibility for them.

You can ask me to show you the personal information I hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, within the limits of the law. For this, or for any question about this policy, please use the contact page or write to me at contact@kuvware.com.
