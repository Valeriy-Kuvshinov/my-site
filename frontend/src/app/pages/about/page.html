The Engineering Difference

  • Security by Discipline

    Trained in technical intelligence and OSINT, I design backends with a zero-trust mindset. I enforce strong authentication to protect your API endpoints and safeguard sensitive business data.

  • Accessibility by Design

    Overlay widgets alone do not make a site compliant or protect you from lawsuits. I write clean, native code built to WCAG 2.1 AA and test it with screen readers and keyboards, so your site is usable by everyone and your legal risk is lower. The reader overlay on this site is an optional comfort tool, not a substitute for accessible code.

  • Infrastructure Independence

    I build systems from the ground up to reduce vendor lock-in. You own your servers, system, and data, and avoid expensive managed-platform subscriptions, keeping your monthly operating costs low.
